About SUMMIT

Started on my parents' roof in Castlegar.

SUMMIT is a locally owned Nelson company that does one thing: permanent lighting, done properly, on homes we drive past every day.

Our Story

Hi, I'm Eric Douglas.

I'm a Nelson local, and SUMMIT started with one install: my parents' house in Castlegar. When I put permanent lights on their roofline, I looked into what companies were charging for the same thing — and couldn't believe the prices.

So I went looking for the best product on the market, found Chromaglow Pro, and decided to do it properly myself. I brought in friends I trust — a carpenter and an electrician — and we got a little obsessed with quality and clean installs: colour-matched track, tidy wiring, and every light seated by hand.

We genuinely enjoy the work, and we love seeing a house transform the first time the lights come on. That's the part that never gets old.

How We Work

What you can expect from us.

You're part of the plan

We design the layout with you on site — down to where the power supply goes — so the finished install looks the way you pictured it.

Clean, careful installs

Colour-matched track, tidy cable routing, and every light seated by hand. We treat your home like our own.

Shown the app before we leave

Backed for 10 years

Commercial-grade Chromaglow Pro hardware with a 10-year comprehensive warranty on parts and workmanship.

Why Chromaglow Pro

We picked the hardware we'd put on our own homes.

Chromaglow Pro is a commercial-grade 48V RGBW system: true warm white from dedicated white diodes, 16 million colours, IP68 waterproofing, UL/cUL certification and a 10-year warranty. It's built for the kind of winters we get here.
