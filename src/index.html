FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

Will I see permanent roofline lights during the day?

Barely. The extruded aluminum track sits low-profile against your roofline and can be custom colour-matched to your trim using RAL paint codes. Multiple track styles suit different mounting scenarios.

How long do permanent Christmas lights last?

The LEDs are rated for 100,000 hours — roughly 45 years at 6 hours of nightly use — and the whole system is backed by a 10-year comprehensive warranty on parts and workmanship.

Can permanent lighting handle Kootenay winters?

That's exactly what it's built for. The system carries an IP68 waterproof rating and UL/cUL certification, with UV-resistant cables rated for 20+ years of outdoor exposure.

Is permanent roofline lighting safe?

Yes. It runs on a 48V low-voltage system — far safer than line voltage — with built-in reverse polarity protection, so even a wiring mistake can't damage the system.

Can I run just warm white, no colours?

Absolutely. Each light has three dedicated white-spectrum diodes separate from the RGB diodes, so you get true, high-quality warm white — not a simulated mix. Many customers run warm white 350 nights a year.

How are the lights controlled?

Through a smartphone app via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Every light is individually addressable — create custom effects, schedule scenes, and control zones independently. Runs up to 300 feet need no extra equipment.

How much do permanent Christmas lights cost in the West Kootenays?

Most homes fall between $4,000 and $11,000 installed, depending on roofline length and access. Use the instant estimate calculator on this page for a range specific to your home, then book a free on-site measure for an exact quote.

How accurate is the instant estimate?

It's based on our real install pricing and is intentionally given as a range. Your exact quote depends on track style, power placement, and roofline details — all confirmed during a free, no-obligation on-site measure.

What's included in the final quote?

A custom-measured layout for your home, total cost, estimated timeline, and full warranty details. No surprises, no pressure.
